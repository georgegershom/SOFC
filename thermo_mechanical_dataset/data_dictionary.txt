DATA DICTIONARY
================================================================================

THERMAL PROPERTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thermal_Conductivity_W_mK: Thermal conductivity (W/m·K)
  - Temperature-dependent, decreases with increasing temperature
  - Reduced by rubber content (insulation effect)
  - Range: ~0.3 to 1.6 W/m·K

Specific_Heat_J_kgK: Specific heat capacity (J/kg·K)
  - Includes moisture evaporation peak at ~100°C
  - Includes dehydration peak at ~450°C
  - Range: ~900 to 2500 J/kg·K

Density_kg_m3: Material density (kg/m³)
  - Decreases with temperature due to moisture loss
  - Reduced by rubber content
  - Range: ~2000 to 2400 kg/m³


MECHANICAL PROPERTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Compressive_Strength_MPa: Uniaxial compressive strength (MPa)
  - Eurocode-based degradation with rubber modification
  - Rubber reduces ambient strength but improves high-temp retention
  - Range: ~5 to 45 MPa

Tensile_Strength_MPa: Uniaxial tensile strength (MPa)
  - Degrades faster than compressive strength
  - Range: ~0.3 to 4.2 MPa

Elastic_Modulus_MPa: Young's modulus (MPa)
  - Significant degradation with temperature
  - Rubber softening effect
  - Range: ~1500 to 35000 MPa

Poisson_Ratio: Poisson's ratio (dimensionless)
  - Increases with temperature due to microcracking
  - Range: 0.15 to 0.35

Fracture_Energy_N_m: Mode I fracture energy (N/m)
  - Decreases with temperature
  - Slightly enhanced by rubber content
  - Range: ~20 to 120 N/m


TRANSPORT PROPERTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gas_Permeability_m2: Intrinsic gas permeability (m²)
  - Exponentially increases with temperature due to thermal damage
  - Increased by rubber content
  - Range: ~1e-17 to 1e-14 m²

Moisture_Diffusivity_m2_s: Moisture diffusion coefficient (m²/s)
  - Increases with temperature and damage
  - Range: ~1e-11 to 1e-8 m²/s

Porosity: Volume fraction of pore space (dimensionless)
  - Increases due to thermal damage and moisture loss
  - Range: 0.12 to 0.25


DEFORMATION PROPERTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thermal_Strain: Free thermal expansion strain (dimensionless)
  - Includes thermal expansion and transient creep
  - Enhanced by rubber content
  - Range: 0 to 0.015

Creep_Coefficient: Load-independent thermal creep coefficient
  - Peaks at intermediate temperatures (500°C)
  - Range: 0.5 to 2.5

Shrinkage_Strain: Autogenous shrinkage strain (dimensionless)
  - Enhanced at elevated temperatures
  - Range: -0.0005 to -0.002
