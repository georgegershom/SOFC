## 🔬 Scientific Findings Embedded

The dataset reflects realistic behavior patterns:

### Mechanical Properties Trends
- ✅ Strength decreases systematically (10% to 38% reduction)
- ✅ Ductility increases dramatically (up to 2.4×)
- ✅ Modulus decreases progressively (up to 47% reduction)
- ✅ Failure mode transitions from brittle to ductile

### Microstructural Insights
- ✅ Weak rubber-cement interface (ITZ: 95-165 μm thick, 35-58% porous)
- ✅ Progressive porosity increase (12.8% → 19.5%)
- ✅ Percolation threshold reached at 15% rubber
- ✅ Reduced C-S-H gel formation near rubber

### Thermal Behavior
- ✅ Rubber decomposes at 300-400°C (peak at 350°C)
- ✅ Enhanced thermal insulation (38% conductivity reduction)
- ✅ Increased heat capacity (15% increase)
- ✅ Slower heat diffusion (43% diffusivity reduction)

### Durability Trade-offs
- ✅ Increased permeability (4.4× at 15% rubber)
- ✅ Improved freeze-thaw resistance (75% less damage)
- ✅ Enhanced impact/ductility resistance
- ✅ Reduced penetration resistance (chloride, carbonation)
