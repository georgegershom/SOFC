================================================================================
PHYSICAL MODELS IMPLEMENTED
================================================================================

1. Strength Degradation
   - Base: Eurocode 2 (EN 1992-1-2)
   - Modification: Rubber content effects
   - Retention at 600°C: C=45%, R10S=50%, R20S=55%

2. Thermal Conductivity
   - Model: Hyperbolic tangent degradation
   - Moisture loss effects included
   - Rubber insulation factor: -35% per 100% rubber

3. Specific Heat
   - Base polynomial trend
   - Moisture evaporation peak at 100°C
   - Dehydration peak at 450°C

4. Permeability
   - Exponential increase with thermal damage
   - k(T) = k₀ · exp(T/300)
   - Rubber increases tortuosity and cracking

5. Elastic Modulus
   - Piecewise linear degradation
   - Rubber softening effect
   - More severe than strength degradation

6. Thermal Strain
   - Linear thermal expansion + transient creep
   - α = (8 + 4T/800) × 10⁻⁶ /K
   - Enhanced by rubber content
