MEASUREMENT TECHNIQUES:
----------------------
1. White Light Interferometry  (Warp)
   - Resolution: ~0.4 mm spatial
   - Accuracy: ±0.05 μm vertical
   - Coverage: Full surface (128×128 grid)

2. Curvature-Based Method (Stoney)
   - Uncertainty: ±12%
   - Type: Non-destructive
   - Output: Layer-averaged stress

3. Layer Removal (Milling)
   - Uncertainty: ±0.05 GPa
   - Type: Destructive
   - Output: Through-thickness profile

4. X-Ray Diffraction (XRD)
   - Uncertainty: ±0.03 GPa
   - Type: Non-destructive (surface only)
   - Resolution: ~1 mm spot

5. Raman Spectroscopy
   - Uncertainty: ±0.08 GPa
   - Type: Non-destructive (surface only)
   - Resolution: 1 μm spot
