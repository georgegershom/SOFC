KEY FEATURES TO OBSERVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. INTERFACE HOTSPOTS (Top region of both panels)
   - Concentrated red/orange zones near anode-electrolyte boundary
   - Driven by coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) mismatch
   - Most critical region for structural integrity

2. NI CLUSTER HOTSPOTS (Scattered throughout)
   - Localized red/yellow spots around Ni nanoparticle agglomerations
   - Result from stress concentration effects
   - Important for predicting coarsening-related degradation

3. BACKGROUND FIELD (Blue/cyan regions)
   - Low baseline crack density in bulk material
   - Slight heterogeneity reflects microstructural variation
   - Textured appearance mimics real Ni/YSZ cermet structure
