## 🔥 **Critical Research Findings**

### **Temperature-Dependent Mechanisms Identified:**

#### **200-250°C: Rubber Softening Phase**
- Thermal expansion creates stress concentrations
- ITZ weakening begins
- Reversible dimensional changes

#### **350-450°C: Rubber Pyrolysis Initiation** 
- Chemical bond breaking begins
- Volatile release creates internal pressure
- Permanent microstructural changes

#### **450-550°C: Portlandite Decomposition**
- Ca(OH)₂ → CaO + H₂O (critical reaction)
- Major strength loss mechanism
- 50% decomposition at ~500°C

#### **600-800°C: Severe Microcracking**
- Calcite decomposition (CO₂ release)
- Thermal mismatch stress peaks
- Network percolation achieved

### **Rubber Content Optimization Guidelines:**
- **5-10%**: High-temperature service applications
- **10-15%**: Balanced performance for moderate temperatures
- **15-20%**: Enhanced fire resistance with acceptable trade-offs
