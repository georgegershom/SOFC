## Phase 1: Comprehensive Material Characterization & Specimen Preparation

### 1.1 Constituent Materials Data (Enhanced for Fire Resistance)

#### **Cement (Fire-Resistant Grade)**
- **Type**: OPC CEM I 42.5R, High-Performance Cement (HPC), or Fire-Resistant Cement
- **Chemical Composition (XRF Analysis)**:
  - SiO₂, Al₂O₃, Fe₂O₃, CaO, MgO, SO₃, K₂O, Na₂O, TiO₂, P₂O₅, MnO, SrO
  - Loss on Ignition (LOI)
  - Free CaO content
- **Bogue Composition**: C₃S, C₂S, C₃A, C₄AF percentages
- **Physical Properties**:
  - Specific gravity (23°C)
  - Blaine fineness (m²/kg)
  - Initial and final setting times
  - Soundness (Le Chatelier method)
- **Fire Resistance Properties**:
  - Thermal conductivity vs. temperature
  - Specific heat capacity vs. temperature
  - Thermal expansion coefficient vs. temperature
  - Dehydration behavior (TGA analysis)

#### **Aggregates (Fire-Resistant Optimized)**
**Coarse Aggregates:**
- **Type**: Crushed granite, basalt, or fire-resistant natural aggregates
- **Grading**: 4.75mm to 20mm (multiple size fractions)
- **Physical Properties**:
  - Specific gravity (SSD and apparent)
  - Water absorption (24h)
  - Bulk density (loose and compacted)
  - Angularity number
  - Flakiness index
- **Thermal Properties**:
  - Thermal conductivity vs. temperature
  - Specific heat capacity vs. temperature
  - Thermal expansion coefficient vs. temperature
  - Fire resistance rating

**Fine Aggregates:**
- **Type**: Natural sand or manufactured sand
- **Grading**: 0.075mm to 4.75mm (full sieve analysis)
- **Physical Properties**: Same as coarse aggregates
- **Thermal Properties**: Same as coarse aggregates

#### **Crumb Rubber (Enhanced Characterization)**
- **Source Specifications**:
  - Tire type (passenger, truck, radial, bias-ply)
  - Manufacturing process (ambient, cryogenic)
  - Age and storage conditions
- **Particle Size Distribution**:
  - Multiple size ranges: 0.075-1mm, 1-2mm, 2-4mm, 4-8mm, 8-12mm
  - Shape analysis (aspect ratio, sphericity)
  - Surface texture (roughness parameters)
- **Physical Properties**:
  - Specific gravity (apparent and bulk)
  - Water absorption
  - Bulk density (loose and compacted)
  - Hardness (Shore A, Mohs scale)
- **Chemical Composition**:
  - **TGA Analysis**: Weight loss vs. temperature (25-800°C)
  - **FTIR Analysis**: Functional groups, polymer type identification
  - **Elemental Analysis**: C, H, N, S, O content
  - **Ash content and composition**
  - **Volatile matter content**
- **Morphological Analysis**:
  - **SEM Images**: Pre-heating and post-heating (200°C, 400°C, 600°C)
  - **Surface area analysis** (BET method)
  - **Pore size distribution**
- **Fire Resistance Properties**:
  - **Ignition temperature**
  - **Heat release rate (cone calorimeter)**
  - **Smoke production rate**
  - **Thermal stability range**
  - **Char formation characteristics**

#### **Water (Quality Control)**
- **pH**: 6.5-8.5 range
- **Impurity Analysis**:
  - Total dissolved solids (TDS)
  - Chloride content
  - Sulfate content
  - Organic matter content
- **Temperature**: 20±2°C

#### **Chemical Admixtures (Fire-Resistant)**
- **Superplasticizer**:
  - Type (polycarboxylate, naphthalene, melamine)
  - Dosage (% by weight of cement)
  - Solid content (%)
  - pH and specific gravity
- **Fire-Resistant Additives**:
  - **Polypropylene fibers**: Length, diameter, dosage
  - **Steel fibers**: Length, diameter, aspect ratio, dosage
  - **Fire-retardant chemicals**: Type, dosage, mechanism
- **Air-Entraining Agent**: Type, dosage, air content achieved

## Phase 3: Thermo-Mechanical Testing Protocol

### 3.1 Specimen Preparation
- **Specimen Types**:
  - Cylinders: 100×200mm, 150×300mm
  - Cubes: 100mm, 150mm
  - Prisms: 100×100×400mm, 150×150×600mm
  - Beams: 150×200×1200mm
  - Slabs: 300×300×50mm, 500×500×100mm
- **Curing Conditions**:
  - Standard curing (20°C, 95% RH)
  - Heat curing (60°C, 80°C)
  - Steam curing
- **Age at Testing**: 7, 14, 28, 56, 90, 180 days

### 3.2 Mechanical Properties Testing

#### **Compressive Strength**
- **Standard Test**: ASTM C39
- **High-Temperature Test**: 20°C, 100°C, 200°C, 300°C, 400°C, 500°C, 600°C, 700°C, 800°C
- **Heating Rate**: 5°C/min, 10°C/min, 20°C/min
- **Loading Rate**: 0.25 MPa/s, 0.5 MPa/s, 1.0 MPa/s
- **Specimen Count**: 6 per condition

#### **Tensile Strength**
- **Splitting Tensile**: ASTM C496
- **Direct Tensile**: Custom setup
- **Temperature Range**: Same as compressive
- **Specimen Count**: 6 per condition

#### **Flexural Strength**
- **Three-Point Bending**: ASTM C78
- **Four-Point Bending**: ASTM C293
- **Temperature Range**: Same as compressive
- **Specimen Count**: 6 per condition

#### **Elastic Modulus**
- **Static Modulus**: ASTM C469
- **Dynamic Modulus**: Resonance frequency method
- **Temperature Range**: Same as compressive
- **Specimen Count**: 6 per condition

#### **Poisson's Ratio**
- **Strain gauge method**
- **Temperature Range**: Same as compressive
- **Specimen Count**: 6 per condition

### 3.3 Thermal Properties Testing

#### **Thermal Conductivity**
- **Hot Wire Method**: ASTM D5930
- **Temperature Range**: 20°C to 800°C
- **Specimen Count**: 3 per mix per temperature

#### **Specific Heat Capacity**
- **Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC)**
- **Temperature Range**: 20°C to 800°C
- **Heating Rate**: 10°C/min
- **Specimen Count**: 3 per mix

#### **Thermal Expansion**
- **Dilatometry**: ASTM E228
- **Temperature Range**: 20°C to 800°C
- **Heating Rate**: 5°C/min
- **Specimen Count**: 3 per mix

#### **Thermal Diffusivity**
- **Laser Flash Method**: ASTM E1461
- **Temperature Range**: 20°C to 800°C
- **Specimen Count**: 3 per mix

### 3.4 Fire Resistance Testing

#### **Fire Resistance Rating**
- **Furnace Test**: ASTM E119
- **Temperature-Time Curve**: ISO 834
- **Duration**: 30, 60, 90, 120, 180 minutes
- **Specimen Count**: 3 per mix per duration

#### **Spalling Resistance**
- **Explosive Spalling Test**
- **Temperature Range**: 200°C to 800°C
- **Heating Rate**: 10°C/min
- **Specimen Count**: 6 per mix

#### **Smoke Production**
- **Smoke Density Chamber**: ASTM E662
- **Temperature Range**: 200°C to 600°C
- **Specimen Count**: 3 per mix
