RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

OPTIMAL RUBBER CONTENT: 10-15%
  • Balances thermal insulation and workability
  • Manageable admixture requirements
  • Expected acceptable strength reduction

ADMIXTURE STRATEGY:
  • Superplasticizer essential above 10% rubber
  • VMA recommended above 10% for segregation control
  • AEA for freeze-thaw resistance

RUBBER SIZE:
  • Fine (1-4mm) preferred for better dispersion
  • Coarse (4-8mm) acceptable but may show slightly lower strength
  • Mixed may offer compromise
