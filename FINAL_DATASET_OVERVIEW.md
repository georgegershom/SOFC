## 🔬 Comprehensive Material Characterization

### 1. Concrete Mixture Proportions ✅
- **Control Mix (0% rubber):** Complete mix design with all constituents
- **Rubberized Mixes (5%, 10%, 15%):** Detailed replacement calculations
- **Constituent Details:** Cement type/grade, aggregate properties, water, admixtures
- **Curing Regime:** 28 days in lime-saturated water at 23°C

### 2. Rubber Aggregate Characterization ✅
- **Source & Type:** Crumb rubber from truck tires (1-4 mm)
- **Physical Properties:** Specific gravity (1.15), water absorption (2.5%), hardness (Shore A 65)
- **Chemical Composition:** Natural rubber (45%), synthetic rubber (35%), carbon black (15%)
- **Thermal Properties:** Decomposition temperatures, residual mass
- **Pre-treatment:** NaOH washing + plasma treatment

### 3. Fresh State Properties ✅
- **Slump Flow:** 50-177 mm (decreases with rubber content)
- **Air Content:** 4.3-4.6% (relatively stable)
- **Fresh Density:** 1280-2465 kg/m³ (decreases with rubber content)
- **Workability Assessment:** Excellent to Poor rating system

### 4. Ambient Temperature Mechanical Properties ✅
- **Compressive Strength (28d):** 15.0-50.1 MPa (decreases with rubber content)
- **Tensile Splitting Strength (28d):** Follows similar degradation pattern
- **Modulus of Elasticity (28d):** Decreases proportionally with rubber content
- **Strength Development:** 7-day and 28-day data included

### 5. Physical Properties ✅
- **Density:** Oven-dry and SSD densities measured
- **Porosity:** 12.0-40.1% (increases with rubber content)
- **Pore Size Distribution:** MIP simulation data included
- **Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity:** Decreases with rubber content
- **Water Absorption:** Characterized for all mixes
