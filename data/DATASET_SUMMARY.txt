USAGE GUIDELINES
================================================================================

1. UNCERTAINTY QUANTIFICATION:
   Run simulations with all three scenarios to bound predictions:
   - Minimum risk: rho0.00.csv (independent, room temp)
   - Maximum risk: HT_uncorrelated.csv (high temp)
   - Intermediate: rho0.50.csv (correlated, room temp)

2. SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS:
   Compare CDF curves to quantify epistemic vs. aleatory uncertainty:
   - Horizontal separation = epistemic (knowledge gap)
   - Curve slope = aleatory (inherent randomness)

3. DESIGN RECOMMENDATIONS:
   - Use ρ=0.5 for conservative room-temp estimates
   - Assume high-T behavior until validated with experiments
   - Target J_applied < 4.0 J/m² for 95% reliability at 800°C

4. EXPERIMENTAL PRIORITIES:
   a) Measure 5-10 co-sintered stacks to determine ρ_real
   b) Perform in-situ 800°C fracture tests (critical!)
   c) Characterize temperature effect on variance (not just mean)
