# Experimental Dataset: Fire-Resistant Rubberized Concrete
## Development and Validation of a Thermo-Mechanical Model for Fire-Resistant Structural Elements

### Dataset Overview
This comprehensive experimental dataset contains results from a systematic investigation of high-performance rubberized concrete behavior under fire conditions. The dataset supports the development and validation of thermo-mechanical models for fire-resistant structural elements.

### Experimental Program Summary

#### Mix Designs
- **Control Mix**: Standard high-strength concrete (HSC)
- **RC-10**: 10% rubber replacement by volume of fine aggregate
- **RC-20**: 20% rubber replacement by volume of fine aggregate
- **RC-30**: 30% rubber replacement by volume of fine aggregate
- **RC-15-SF**: 15% rubber + 8% silica fume
- **RC-15-FA**: 15% rubber + 15% fly ash

#### Testing Conditions
- **Curing Ages**: 7, 28, 56 days
- **Test Temperatures**: 23°C, 200°C, 400°C, 600°C, 800°C
- **Cooling Regimes**: Furnace cooling (slow) and water quenching (rapid)
- **Specimens per Condition**: Minimum 3 for statistical significance

#### Test Standards
- Compressive Strength: ASTM C39
- Tensile Strength: ASTM C496 (splitting) and ASTM C78 (flexural)
- Elastic Modulus: ASTM C469
- Density and UPV: ASTM C597

### Data Quality and Validation
- All tests performed in triplicate minimum
- Statistical analysis included (mean, standard deviation, coefficient of variation)
- Quality control measures documented
- Calibration certificates for all equipment included
