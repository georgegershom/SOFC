
# Microstructural Analysis Dataset Summary Report

## Dataset Overview
- **Total Records**: 18,682
- **Number of Datasets**: 16
- **Analysis Techniques**: SEM, XRD, TGA/DTA, Micro-CT

## Key Findings

### Portlandite Decomposition
- **Critical Temperature**: 500°C (50% decomposition)
- **Temperature Range**: 450-550°C
- **Rubber Effect**: Minimal impact on decomposition temperature

### Porosity Evolution
- **Porosity Range**: 0.120 - 0.600
- **Maximum Increase**: 400.0%
- **Rubber Effect**: Increases baseline porosity

### Mass Loss Analysis
- **Mass Loss Range**: 15.0% - 34.2%
- **Major Events**: Free water, C-S-H dehydration, Portlandite decomposition
- **Rubber Contribution**: Significant at high rubber contents

## Critical Temperature Ranges

1. **200-250°C**: Rubber softening and thermal expansion
2. **350-450°C**: Rubber pyrolysis initiation
3. **450-550°C**: Portlandite decomposition
4. **600-800°C**: Calcite decomposition and severe microcracking

## Recommendations

- **Low Temperature Service (< 200°C)**: 15-20% rubber content acceptable
- **Moderate Temperature Service (200-400°C)**: 10-15% rubber content recommended
- **High Temperature Service (> 400°C)**: 5-10% rubber content maximum
