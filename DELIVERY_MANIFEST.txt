📊 EXPERIMENTAL DATA GENERATED:
   
   1. FLOW REGIME CHARACTERIZATION
      • 100 unique experimental conditions
      • Parameters: void fraction (α), superficial velocities (U_SG, U_SL)
      • Flow patterns: smooth stratified (15), wavy stratified (85)
      • Interface properties: height, wave amplitude, wave frequency
      • Operating ranges:
        - Void fraction: 0.78 - 0.95
        - Gas velocity: 0.76 - 14.95 m/s
        - Liquid velocity: 0.015 - 0.487 m/s
        - Temperature: 15 - 30 °C
        - Pressure: 1.0 - 3.0 bar

   2. ACOUSTIC SIGNAL TRANSMISSION
      • 600 attenuation measurements (6 frequencies × 100 experiments)
      • Frequencies tested: 100, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000, 10000 Hz
      • Time-series data: 10 experiments at 51.2 kHz sampling
      • Signal duration: 1 second per recording
      • Measurements:
        - Source and received signal amplitudes
        - Frequency spectra (FFT-ready)
        - Signal-to-Noise Ratio (5-46 dB range)
        - Phase and mixture sound speeds

   3. ATTENUATION METRICS
      • Attenuation coefficient (γ): 0.0001 - 4.72 Np/m
      • Transmission loss: calculated for 5m pipe
      • Frequency-dependent attenuation profiles
      • Mechanism breakdown:
        * Viscous absorption contribution
        * Scattering contribution (interface waves)
        * Turbulence-induced contribution

   4. FLUID PROPERTIES & CONDITIONS
      • Gas phase (Air):
        - Density: pressure/temperature dependent
        - Viscosity: temperature dependent
        - Sound speed: 340-345 m/s
      • Liquid phase (Water):
        - Density: 996-998 kg/m³
        - Viscosity: temperature dependent
        - Sound speed: 1470-1480 m/s
      • All properties calculated from first principles

   5. TURBULENCE & SHEAR LAYER DATA
      • Reynolds numbers: gas (Re_G) and liquid (Re_L)
      • Turbulent kinetic energy (TKE): both phases
      • Dissipation rate (ε): both phases
      • Shear stress: wall (gas & liquid) and interfacial
      • Friction velocities: both phases
      • Friction factors: both phases
      • Velocity profiles: 20 radial positions × 2 phases × 100 experiments
